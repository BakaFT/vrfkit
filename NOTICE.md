### What is derived

| Area | Relationship |
|---|---|
| `crates/vrf-transform` | The shared payload-transform framework and six global per-build implementations are ports of `Replay.Encoding/PayloadEncryption`; substitution tables and global golden vectors are extracted mechanically from that source (`tools/extract_sboxes.py`, `tools/extract_golden.py`). Regional provenance is stated separately below. |
| `crates/vrf-bitio` | The Unreal wire primitives (`IntPacked`, bounded `SerializedInt`, `FString`, bit copying) follow the semantics implemented in `Replay.Encoding/Archives`. |

The payload-transform framework and the reverse-engineering approach originate
with that project; this repository reimplements and extends the result.

## ValorantReplayParser -- LeagueTavern fork

`versions::V13_02China`'s 64-bit lane and its three constants come from the
`china-server-support` branch of
[LeagueTavern/ValorantReplayParser](https://github.com/LeagueTavern/ValorantReplayParser),
also MIT. That branch documents them as recovered by decompiling a third party's
private build.

Its 32-bit and byte lanes are **not** derived from that source, which left both
zeroing their input, and are original to this repository -- see the type's doc
comment for how they were derived and what they were measured against.

## China 13.04 replay recovery

`versions::V13_04China` is original to this repository. Its word functions and
constants were recovered from replay measurements, then checked against a real
285,488-block replay and an independent Python implementation. It reuses the
shared transform staging and PRNG framework described above; its per-build
values were not extracted from ValorantReplayParser. The 11 regional golden
vectors are generated by `tools/china_transform_oracle.py`.
